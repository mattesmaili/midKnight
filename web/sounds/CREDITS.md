# Sound credits

All recordings are CC0 1.0 (public domain) from Freesound:

- `move.mp3` and the knock in `check.mp3`, `mate.mp3`, `draw.mp3`: one hit cut
  from "chess pieces.wav" by simone_ds, https://freesound.org/s/366065/
- `capture.mp3`: one hit cut from "Chess_foley_1.mp3" by Amatsuuu,
  https://freesound.org/s/629182/

The woodblock ticks in `check.mp3` and the chimes in `mate.mp3`, `draw.mp3`
and `start.mp3` were synthesized for this project.

To use other sounds, replace a file and keep its name.
